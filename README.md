## About me

I am a Computer Science Education student at **Universitas Pendidikan Indonesia (FPMIPA)** who enjoys turning ideas into useful digital experiences.

My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering, educational technology, and game development**. I build web and mobile applications, explore AI-assisted learning products, and develop interactive experiences that make learning more engaging.

- 🎓 Bachelor of Education in Computer Science Education — UPI, 2023–present
- 📚 GPA: **3.92 / 4.00**
- 👨‍🏫 Former Data Structure Practicum Assistant for **40+ students**
- 🎮 Game programmer in educational research and Unity-based projects
- 🏆 LIDM 2025 Finalist and Beasiswa Unggulan 2024 Awardee
- 📍 Cianjur / Bandung, Indonesia
